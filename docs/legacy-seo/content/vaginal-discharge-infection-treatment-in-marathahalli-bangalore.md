## Understanding Vaginal Discharge – Normal vs. Abnormal

Vaginal discharge is a natural way for the body to keep the vagina clean and free from infections. However, changes in color, texture, or smell can indicate an underlying infection or health issue.

![Youthful Indian Woman](https://neofertility.co.in/wp-content/uploads/Youthful-Indian-Woman.jpg)

### Normal Vaginal Discharge:

**✅ Clear or white in color.**

**✅ Odorless or mild smell.**

**✅ Sticky or watery texture (changes during the menstrual cycle).**

### Abnormal Vaginal Discharge – Signs of Infection:

**✅ Unusual color – Yellow, green, gray, or brown.**

**✅ Foul or fishy smell.**

**✅ Thick, cottage cheese-like, or frothy texture.**

**✅ Accompanied by itching, burning, or irritation.**

## What Causes Vaginal Infections?

Vaginal infections can be caused by bacteria, fungi, viruses, or imbalances in vaginal flora. Identifying the cause helps in choosing the right treatment.

![bacteria](https://neofertility.co.in/wp-content/uploads/noun-bacteria-7627114-326F7D.svg)

#### **1️⃣ Bacterial Vaginosis (BV)**

✔️ **Overgrowth of harmful bacteria** leading to a fishy-smelling discharge.

✔️ **Common after unprotected sex or excessive douching**.

![yeast](https://neofertility.co.in/wp-content/uploads/noun-yeast-6058661-326F7D.svg)

### **2️⃣ Yeast Infection (Candidiasis)**

✔️ **Caused by Candida fungus** – Leads to **itching, redness, and thick white discharge**.

✔️ **Triggered by antibiotics, diabetes, hormonal changes, or weak immunity**.

![std icon](https://neofertility.co.in/wp-content/uploads/noun-std-4846685-326F7D.svg)

### **3️⃣ Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs) like Chlamydia, Gonorrhea, Trichomoniasis**

✔️ **STIs can cause abnormal discharge, pain, and swelling**.

✔️ **Trichomoniasis results in foamy, yellow-green discharge**.

![vaginal infection](https://neofertility.co.in/wp-content/uploads/noun-vaginal-infections-5911873-326F7D.svg)

### **4️⃣ Vaginal Atrophy (Menopausal Dryness)**

✔️ **Reduced estrogen levels lead to dryness, discomfort, and infections**.

✔️ **Common in postmenopausal women**.

## How is Vaginal Infection Diagnosed?

A gynecologist will conduct a pelvic exam, swab test, and lab analysis to determine the type of infection and recommend the best treatment.

![Indian Female](https://neofertility.co.in/wp-content/uploads/Indian-Female.jpg)

### Quick & Reliable Testing & Diagnosis:

**✅ Pelvic Examination**– To check for inflammation or abnormal discharge.

**✅ pH Test**– A high vaginal pH may indicate bacterial vaginosis or trichomoniasis.

**✅ Microscopic Analysis**– Detects yeast, bacteria, or parasites.

**✅ STI Screening**– For infections like gonorrhea, chlamydia, or herpes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) About Vaginal Infections

#### 1️⃣ When should I see a doctor for vaginal discharge?

If you have **abnormal discharge (yellow, green, gray), foul smell, itching, burning, or pelvic pain**, seek medical advice immediately.

#### 2️⃣ Can vaginal infections go away on their own?

Mild infections **may resolve on their own**, but **bacterial and fungal infections need medical treatment** to prevent complications.

#### 3️⃣ Can poor hygiene cause vaginal infections?

✔️ **Yes.** Using scented soaps, douching, or wearing tight synthetic underwear can **disrupt vaginal flora and cause infections**.

#### 4️⃣ Can vaginal infections affect fertility?

✔️ **Yes.** Untreated infections **can lead to PID, fallopian tube damage, and infertility**.

#### 5️⃣ Can I get a vaginal infection even if I’m not sexually active?

✔️ **Yes.** Vaginal infections **can be caused by bacteria overgrowth, hormonal changes, antibiotics, or poor hygiene**.

#### 6️⃣ How long does it take to treat a vaginal infection?

✔️ **Mild infections improve within a few days** with the right medication. **Chronic or recurrent infections may take longer** and require lifestyle changes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) About STIs

#### 1️⃣ How do I know if I have an STI?

Some STIs **do not show symptoms**, while others cause **pain, discharge, genital sores, or fever**. **Regular STI testing** is the best way to confirm an infection.

#### 2️⃣ Can STIs be cured?

✔️ **Bacterial STIs like Chlamydia, Gonorrhea, and Syphilis** are **curable with antibiotics**.

✔️ **Viral STIs like Herpes and HIV** cannot be cured but **can be managed with antiviral medications**.

#### 3️⃣ How often should I get tested for STIs?

✔️ If sexually active, **get tested at least once a year**.

✔️ If you have **multiple partners or unprotected sex**, test **every 3-6 months**.

#### 4️⃣ Can I get an STI even if I don’t have penetrative sex?

Yes. **STIs like herpes, HPV, and pubic lice can spread through skin-to-skin contact** or oral sex.

#### 5️⃣ Can I get pregnant if I have an STI?

Some untreated STIs **can cause infertility or complications during pregnancy**. If planning pregnancy, **get tested and treated early**.

#### 6️⃣ Is STI testing confidential?

Yes! Our **STI tests and treatments are 100% confidential**, and your privacy is protected at all times.

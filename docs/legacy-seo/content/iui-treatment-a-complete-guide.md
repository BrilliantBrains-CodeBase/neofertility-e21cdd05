When couples walk into my clinic after months or years of trying to conceive, one of the most common questions I hear is, _“Doctor, do we need IVF?”_

My answer is often, “Not necessarily.”

For many couples, [Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)](https://neofertility.co.in/intrauterine-insemination-iui-treatment-marathahalli-whitefield-in-bangalore/ " Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)") can be an effective first step before considering more advanced fertility treatments. IUI is one of the most commonly performed fertility procedures worldwide because it is less invasive, more affordable than IVF, and can significantly improve pregnancy chances in selected patients.

In this guide, I will explain who should consider IUI, how the procedure works, what success rates you can realistically expect, how much IUI costs in Bangalore, and when it may be time to move to IVF.

## What Is IUI Treatment?

IUI, or Intrauterine Insemination, is a fertility treatment in which specially prepared sperm is placed directly inside the uterus around the time of ovulation.

In natural conception, sperm must travel through the cervix, uterus, and fallopian tubes before reaching the egg. [During IUI](https://neofertility.co.in/intrauterine-insemination-iui-treatment-marathahalli-whitefield-in-bangalore/ "During IUI"), we bypass several of these barriers and place the healthiest sperm closer to the egg, increasing the chances of fertilization.

Unlike IVF, fertilization still occurs naturally inside the body.

At Neo Fertility Clinic, I often recommend IUI as a first-line treatment for couples with mild fertility issues because it is relatively simple, safe, and cost-effective.

* * *

## Who Should Consider IUI?

One of the first things I tell my patients is that IUI is not suitable for everyone. Success largely depends on selecting the right candidates.

### Couples with Unexplained Infertility

Sometimes all fertility tests appear normal, yet pregnancy does not occur. We call this unexplained infertility.

In such situations, IUI can improve the number of sperm reaching the egg and may increase the chances of conception.

### Mild Male Factor Infertility

If the [semen analysis](https://neofertility.co.in/best-male-infertility-treatment-in-marathahalli-bangalore/ "semen analysis") shows mildly reduced sperm count or motility, sperm preparation techniques used during IUI can help select the healthiest sperm for insemination.

### Women with Ovulation Disorders

Women with conditions such as PCOS often benefit from ovulation induction combined with IUI.

### Cervical Factor Infertility

Occasionally, cervical mucus may interfere with sperm movement. Since IUI bypasses the cervix, it can overcome this problem effectively.

### Mild Endometriosis

Women with early-stage endometriosis may achieve pregnancy through IUI before considering IVF.

### Donor Sperm Treatments

IUI is also commonly used with [donor sperm](https://neofertility.co.in/donor-eggs-and-sperm-program-in-marathahalli-bangalore/ "donor sperm ") in appropriate situations.

* * *

## Who Is Not an Ideal Candidate for IUI?

In my practice, I generally advise patients to consider [IVF instead of IUI](https://neofertility.co.in/best-ivf-treatment-in-marathahalli-whitefield-bangalore/ "IVF instead of IUI") when:

- Both fallopian tubes are blocked
- Severe male factor infertility is present
- Advanced endometriosis is diagnosed
- Ovarian reserve is significantly reduced
- Multiple IUI cycles have already failed
- Maternal age is advanced and time is critical

The key is choosing the treatment that gives the highest chance of success rather than simply starting with the least invasive option.

* * *

## What Tests Do I Recommend Before IUI?

Before beginning any fertility treatment, I perform a comprehensive fertility assessment for both partners.

### Tests for Women

Typically, I recommend:

- AMH test for ovarian reserve
- FSH and LH hormone testing
- Thyroid profile
- Prolactin testing
- Pelvic ultrasound
- Fallopian tube assessment through HSG or SSG

One of the most important requirements for IUI is that at least one fallopian tube should be open because fertilization still occurs naturally.

### Tests for Men

The most important investigation is a detailed [semen analysis.](https://neofertility.co.in/best-male-infertility-treatment-in-marathahalli-bangalore/ "semen analysis.")

This helps us evaluate:

- Sperm count
- Sperm motility
- Sperm morphology
- Semen volume

Additional tests may sometimes be required depending on the results.

* * *

## How Does the IUI Process Work?

Many couples imagine fertility treatments to be complicated and stressful. Fortunately, IUI is relatively straightforward.

### Step 1: Ovulation Monitoring

We begin by monitoring the woman’s menstrual cycle.

Depending on the situation, I may prescribe medications such as:

- Letrozole
- Clomiphene Citrate
- Gonadotropin injections

The goal is to encourage the development of one or more mature follicles.

### Step 2: Trigger Injection

Once the follicles reach the appropriate size, we administer an ovulation trigger injection.

This helps us predict exactly when ovulation will occur and schedule the insemination accordingly.

### Step 3: Sperm Preparation

On the day of the procedure, the semen sample is processed in our laboratory.

This is one of the most important stages of treatment.

* * *

## How Is Sperm Prepared for IUI?

Patients are often surprised to learn that we do not use the semen sample directly.

Instead, the sample undergoes a process called sperm washing.

During sperm washing, we separate:

- Healthy sperm
- Highly motile sperm

From:

- Seminal fluid
- Debris
- Dead sperm
- Immobile sperm

The result is a concentrated sample containing the strongest sperm, which can significantly improve the chances of fertilization.

* * *

## What Happens During the IUI Procedure?

One of the biggest concerns patients have is whether the procedure will be painful.

The good news is that IUI is usually quick and well tolerated.

During the procedure:

1. You lie comfortably on the examination table.
2. A speculum is gently inserted.
3. A thin catheter is passed through the cervix.
4. The prepared sperm sample is deposited inside the uterus.

The entire procedure usually takes only a few minutes.

Most of my patients return to work and normal activities on the same day.

* * *

## Is IUI Painful?

In my experience, most women describe IUI as mildly uncomfortable rather than painful.

You may experience:

- Mild cramping
- Temporary pelvic discomfort
- Light spotting

The sensation is often comparable to a Pap smear examination.

Significant pain is uncommon.

* * *

## What Are the Success Rates of IUI?

This is probably the question I hear most frequently in my consultation room.

The honest answer is that success rates vary depending on several factors.

### Age Matters

Female age remains one of the strongest predictors of success.

Generally, younger women have higher pregnancy rates because egg quality tends to be better.

### Cause of Infertility

IUI performs best in:

- Unexplained infertility
- Mild male factor infertility
- Ovulation disorders

### Sperm Quality

Higher sperm count and better motility generally improve outcomes.

### Ovarian Reserve

Women with healthy ovarian reserve often achieve better results.

### Realistic Success Rates

In general, pregnancy rates per cycle range from approximately:

- 10–20% per cycle in younger women
- 5–10% per cycle in women above 38 years

I always encourage patients to look at cumulative success rates rather than focusing on a single cycle.

* * *

## How Many IUI Cycles Do I Recommend?

Most fertility specialists, including myself, recommend:

- Up to 3 cycles for women above 35 years
- Up to 6 cycles for younger women

Most successful IUI pregnancies occur within the first few treatment cycles.

If pregnancy does not occur despite properly timed cycles, it may be time to consider IVF.

* * *

## How Can You Improve IUI Success?

Although no treatment can guarantee pregnancy, there are several ways to optimize outcomes.

### Maintain a Healthy Weight

Both underweight and overweight patients may experience reduced fertility.

### Follow Fertility Medications Correctly

Proper medication use improves follicular development and timing.

### Optimize Sperm Health

I advise male partners to:

- Avoid smoking
- Limit alcohol
- Maintain healthy sleep habits
- Exercise regularly
- Reduce stress

### Manage Existing Medical Conditions

Conditions such as thyroid disorders, diabetes, and PCOS should be well controlled before treatment.

### Choose an Experienced Fertility Team

Accurate diagnosis, cycle monitoring, laboratory quality, and individualized treatment protocols all influence success rates.

* * *

## What Is the Cost of IUI in Bangalore?

The cost of IUI in Bangalore varies depending on:

- Fertility medications used
- Number of monitoring scans required
- Laboratory procedures
- Individual fertility needs

Typically, an IUI cycle may range between ₹8,000 and ₹25,000 or more.

Additional costs may include:

- Fertility investigations
- Hormonal injections
- Ultrasound monitoring
- Blood tests

During consultations, I always provide patients with a transparent estimate so they can plan their treatment comfortably.

* * *

## When Should Couples Move to IVF?

One of the most important decisions in fertility treatment is knowing when to move from IUI to IVF.

I usually recommend IVF when:

- Three to six IUI cycles have failed
- Severe male factor infertility exists
- Fallopian tubes are blocked
- Ovarian reserve is significantly reduced
- Maternal age is advanced
- Endometriosis is severe

In these situations, IVF generally offers a substantially higher chance of pregnancy.

* * *

## My Final Thoughts

As a fertility specialist, I believe IUI remains an excellent treatment option for carefully selected couples. It is simple, minimally invasive, and considerably more affordable than IVF.

However, the success of IUI depends on proper patient selection, accurate diagnosis, precise timing, and individualized treatment planning.

At Neo Fertility Clinic, my goal is to help every couple choose the fertility treatment that offers the best chance of achieving a healthy pregnancy while avoiding unnecessary delays.

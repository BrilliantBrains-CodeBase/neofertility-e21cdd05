## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) About Donor Egg and Sperm Programs

Find answers to the most frequently asked questions about Donor Egg & Sperm program in Marathahalli - Whitefield in Bangalore.

#### 1️⃣ Is using donor eggs or sperm legal in India?

Yes, **egg and sperm donation is legal in India**, regulated under **strict medical and ethical guidelines**, ensuring confidentiality and safety for both donors and recipients.

#### 2️⃣ How are donors screened for the program?

All donors undergo a **rigorous screening process**, including:

✔️ **Medical history review** to rule out genetic disorders.

✔️ **Fertility tests** to confirm egg or sperm quality.

✔️ **Infectious disease screening** to ensure donor health.

✔️ **Psychological evaluation** to assess emotional well-being.

#### 3️⃣ Can I choose my egg or sperm donor?

Yes, intended parents can **specify preferences** such as **physical traits, ethnicity, and medical background**, and donors are carefully matched accordingly.

#### 4️⃣ What are the success rates of using donor eggs or sperm?

Success rates depend on:

✔️ **Age of the recipient.**

✔️ **Egg or sperm quality.**

✔️ **The number of embryos transferred.**

In most cases, **IVF with donor eggs or sperm has a success rate of 70-80%**.

#### 5️⃣ Will the child inherit traits from the donor?

Yes, **the child will inherit genetic traits from the donor**, but intended parents **select donors based on physical and genetic compatibility**.

#### 6️⃣ Is the identity of the donor disclosed?

No, **donor identity remains anonymous**, ensuring confidentiality for both donors and recipients.

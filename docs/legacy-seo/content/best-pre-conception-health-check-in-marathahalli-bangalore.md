## Is Pre-Conception Counselling Right for You?

Whether you are trying to conceive naturally or planning fertility treatments, a pre-conception check-up can help optimize your chances of a healthy pregnancy.

#### You should consider a pre-conception check-up if:

✅ You are planning to get pregnant within the next **6-12 months**.

✅ You have **irregular menstrual cycles, PCOS, thyroid issues, or hormonal imbalances**.

✅ You or your partner have **existing medical conditions like diabetes, hypertension, or genetic disorders**.

✅ You have had **previous miscarriages or difficulty conceiving**.

✅ You want to **reduce pregnancy risks and ensure a smooth conception journey**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) About Pre-Conception Counselling

#### 1️⃣What is the best time to get a pre-conception check-up?

It’s best to **schedule a pre-conception check-up at least 3-6 months before trying to conceive**.

#### 2️⃣ Can a pre-conception check-up improve fertility?

Yes! Addressing health concerns **before conception can improve fertility, regulate cycles, and boost pregnancy success rates**.

#### 3️⃣ Is pre-conception counselling necessary if I’ve had a baby before?

Yes, even if you’ve had a child before, **your health and fertility change over time**, making a **pre-conception check-up beneficial for future pregnancies**.

#### 4️⃣ Can pre-conception counselling help prevent pregnancy complications?

Absolutely! By identifying and treating **underlying conditions like diabetes, thyroid issues, and infections**, you can reduce the risk of **miscarriage, preterm labor, and pregnancy complications**.

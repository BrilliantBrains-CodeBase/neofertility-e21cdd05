## Understanding MTP – A Safe & Legal Abortion Option

Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) is a safe, legal method for ending an unintended or medically necessary pregnancy using medications or minor surgical procedures, under expert medical supervision.

![Medico legal](https://neofertility.co.in/wp-content/uploads/Medico-Legal.jpg)

**✅Legal in India up to 24 weeks of pregnancy (as per MTP Act).**

**✅Non-surgical method (Abortion Pills) available up to 9 weeks of pregnancy.**

**✅Surgical options (D&C, Vacuum Aspiration) for pregnancies beyond 9 weeks.**

**✅Completely confidential & performed by certified gynecologists.**

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) About MTP

#### 1️⃣ Is abortion legal in India?

Yes, abortion is **legal in India** under the **MTP Act**, allowing termination up to **24 weeks of pregnancy** under specific conditions.

#### 2️⃣ Is medical abortion safe?

Yes, when performed under medical supervision, **MTP using abortion pills is safe and 98% effective** for pregnancies up to **9 weeks**.

#### 3️⃣ How long does it take to recover from a surgical abortion?

Most women recover **within 24-48 hours** after a **vacuum aspiration or D&E procedure**. Mild cramping or spotting may occur.

#### 4️⃣ Will abortion affect future fertility?

No, **safe and legal abortions do not impact fertility** when performed by trained professionals.

#### 5️⃣ How confidential is the abortion procedure?

All MTP procedures are **100% confidential**. **No personal details are disclosed**.

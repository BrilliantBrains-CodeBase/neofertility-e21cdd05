## Understanding PID – Causes, Symptoms & Risk Factors

Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID) is an infection of the female reproductive organs, often caused by untreated sexually transmitted infections (STIs) like chlamydia and gonorrhea or bacterial infections from childbirth or surgery.

![Happy Indian Women Smiling](https://neofertility.co.in/wp-content/uploads/Happy-Indian-WOmen-SMiling.jpg)

### Common Causes of PID:

**✅** Bacterial infections from STIs or vaginal infections. **✅** Untreated chlamydia or gonorrhea spreading to reproductive organs. **✅** IUD insertion, childbirth, miscarriage, or abortion leading to bacterial exposure. **✅** Multiple sexual partners or unprotected sex, increasing the risk of infections.

### Symptoms of PID:

**✅** Lower abdominal or pelvic pain. **✅** Foul-smelling vaginal discharge. **✅** Pain during urination or intercourse. **✅** Irregular periods or heavy bleeding. **✅** Fever, chills, or nausea.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) About Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID)

#### 1️⃣ Can PID be cured completely?

Yes, **early diagnosis and proper treatment with antibiotics can cure PID**. However, **delayed treatment may lead to permanent reproductive damage**.

#### 2️⃣ How do I know if I have PID?

Common symptoms include **pelvic pain, unusual vaginal discharge, fever, painful intercourse, and irregular periods**. If you experience these, seek medical help immediately.

#### 3️⃣ Can I get PID without an STI?

Yes! **Not all cases of PID are caused by STIs.** Other causes include **post-surgical infections, bacterial overgrowth, or untreated vaginal infections.**

#### 4️⃣ What happens if PID is left untreated?

Untreated PID can lead to **chronic pelvic pain, infertility, ectopic pregnancy, and long-term reproductive damage.**

#### 5️⃣ How can I prevent PID?

✔️ **Practice safe sex (use condoms)**.

✔️ **Get regular STI screenings** if sexually active.

✔️ **Avoid douching**, which disrupts vaginal bacteria balance.

#### 6️⃣ Can I get pregnant after having PID?

If treated early, **many women with PID can conceive naturally**. However, **severe cases may require fertility treatments** to restore reproductive function.

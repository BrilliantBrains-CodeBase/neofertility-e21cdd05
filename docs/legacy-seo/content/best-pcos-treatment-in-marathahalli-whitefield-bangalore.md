## What is PCOS?

![Indian Woman With Crossed Fingers](https://neofertility.co.in/wp-content/uploads/Indian-Woman-with-crossed-fingers.jpg)

**PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)** is a hormonal disorder affecting women of reproductive age. It can cause irregular periods, excessive hair growth, weight gain, and even infertility.

The good news? With the right treatment and lifestyle modifications, PCOS can be effectively managed.

PCOS occurs when the ovaries produce excessive amounts of androgens (male hormones), leading to an imbalance that affects ovulation, metabolism, and overall well-being.

Women with PCOS often have multiple small cysts on their ovaries, but not every woman with PCOS has cysts.

#### Diagnostic Methods:

✅ **Detailed Medical History & Symptom Analysis** – Evaluating your menstrual cycle, weight changes, and other symptoms.

✅ **Pelvic Ultrasound (Transvaginal or Abdominal Scan)** – Checking for ovarian cysts and assessing ovarian function.

✅ **Hormonal Blood Tests** – Assessing levels of **LH, FSH, testosterone, estrogen, and progesterone**.

✅ **Insulin & Glucose Tolerance Test** – PCOS is often linked with insulin resistance and increased diabetes risk.

✅ **Thyroid Function Test** – To rule out other hormonal imbalances that mimic PCOS.

## Frequently Asked Questions About PCOS Treatment

Find answers to the most frequently asked questions about PCOS Treatment in Marathahalli - Whitefield in Bangalore.

#### 1️⃣Can PCOS be cured permanently?

PCOS is a lifelong condition, but its symptoms can be managed effectively with the right **diet, exercise, and medications**. Long-term management helps prevent complications like diabetes and infertility.

#### 2️⃣What is the best diet for PCOS?

A **low-carb, high-fiber diet** rich in vegetables, lean proteins, and whole grains can help **balance hormones and improve metabolism**.

#### 3️⃣ Can PCOS cause infertility?

Yes, PCOS can affect ovulation, but **with the right treatment, most women with PCOS can conceive naturally or through assisted reproductive techniques.**

#### 4️⃣ How is PCOS different from PCOD?

PCOS is a more severe metabolic condition that affects the ovaries and other body systems, whereas PCOD primarily refers to **cyst formation on the ovaries**.

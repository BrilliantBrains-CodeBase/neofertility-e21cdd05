## What Causes Infertility?

Infertility can affect both men and women, and knowing the cause is the first step toward successful treatment.

Infertility isn’t always a single issue—it can be caused by hormonal imbalances, structural problems, or lifestyle factors. Our specialists at Neo Fertility Clinic perform comprehensive evaluations to identify the exact reason and create a personalized treatment plan.

### Causes of Infertility in Women:

✅ **Ovulation Disorders** – Irregular or absent ovulation due to PCOS, thyroid issues, or hormonal imbalances.

✅ **Blocked Fallopian Tubes** – Tubal damage from infections, previous surgeries, or endometriosis.

✅ **Endometriosis** – A condition where tissue similar to the uterine lining grows outside the uterus, affecting fertility.

✅ **Poor Egg Quality** – Age-related decline in egg health or premature ovarian failure.

✅ **Uterine Abnormalities** – Fibroids, polyps, or scar tissue that may prevent implantation.

✅ **Hormonal Imbalances** – Issues with estrogen, progesterone, or thyroid hormones affecting conception.

### Causes of Infertility in Men:

✅ **Low Sperm Count** – Fewer sperm reduce the chances of fertilization.

✅ **Poor Sperm Motility** – Sperm that cannot swim properly may struggle to reach the egg.

✅ **Abnormal Sperm Shape (Morphology)** – Irregular sperm shape affects fertilization.

✅ **Hormonal Imbalances** – Low testosterone levels can impact sperm production.

✅ **Varicocele** – Enlarged veins in the scrotum affecting sperm quality.

✅ **Infections & Lifestyle Factors** – Smoking, alcohol, obesity, and stress can affect fertility.

**Did you know?** In nearly 30-40% of infertility cases, both partners may have contributing factors. That’s why a complete fertility evaluation for both men and women is essential!

## Comprehensive Fertility Evaluation

**Find Out What’s Holding You Back – Fertility Evaluation That Gives You Answers.**

Before starting any treatment, it’s important to understand the root cause of infertility. At Neo Fertility Clinic, we conduct a detailed evaluation using advanced tests to create a personalized plan for you.

![Indian Female](https://neofertility.co.in/wp-content/uploads/Indian-Female.jpg)

### Fertility Evaluation for Women:

✅ **Hormonal Blood Tests** – Checking levels of key reproductive hormones like FSH, LH, AMH, and thyroid hormones.

✅ **Pelvic Ultrasound Scan** – Examining ovaries, uterus, and follicle count.

✅ **Ovulation Tracking** – Understanding your cycle and the best time to conceive.

✅ **HSG (Hysterosalpingogram) or Sonohysterogram** – Checking if fallopian tubes are open.

✅ **Endometrial Health Assessment** – Ensuring the uterus is ready for implantation.

### Fertility Evaluation for Men:

✅ **Semen Analysis** – Testing sperm count, motility, and morphology.

✅ **Hormonal Testing** – Checking testosterone and other hormones affecting fertility.

✅ **Physical Examination** – Identifying any structural or medical issues affecting sperm health.

## FAQs About Infertility & Treatment

Got questions about fertility treatments? We’ve got answers! Below are the most common questions about infertility evaluation and treatment in Marathahalli - Whitefield in Bangalore to help you make informed decisions.

#### When should I see a fertility specialist?

If you have been trying to conceive for over a year (or 6 months if you’re over 35) without success, it's best to consult a specialist for an infertility evaluation.

#### What are the first steps in infertility evaluation?

The first step involves a detailed consultation, blood tests, ultrasound for women, and semen analysis for men to identify potential fertility issues.

#### What treatments are available for infertility?

We offer a wide range of personalized fertility treatments including ovulation induction, IUI, IVF, ICSI, egg freezing, and surgical procedures like laparoscopy & hysteroscopy to improve conception chances.

#### Is IVF my only option if I’m struggling to conceive?

No! Many couples conceive through ovulation tracking, lifestyle changes, IUI, or hormonal therapy before considering IVF. Our specialists recommend the most suitable treatment for your condition.

#### What are the success rates of fertility treatments?

Success rates depend on age, diagnosis, and treatment type. For instance, IVF has a 40-60% success rate per cycle, while IUI has 10-20% per cycle. Your doctor will guide you based on your individual case.

#### Is male infertility treatable?

Yes! Male infertility can often be treated with lifestyle changes, medication, hormonal therapy, or advanced techniques like ICSI or sperm retrieval procedures (TESA/PESA).

#### How much does infertility treatment cost?

The cost varies depending on the treatment required. We offer affordable and transparent pricing for infertility evaluation and treatment in Marathahalli - Whitefield in Bangalore. Contact us for a consultation to get a detailed cost estimate.

#### Can I still conceive naturally after fertility treatment?

Yes! Many couples who undergo fertility treatments improve their reproductive health and successfully conceive naturally in the future.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) About Pregnancy Care

#### 1️⃣ How often should I see my doctor during pregnancy?

In the first trimester, **monthly check-ups** are recommended. In the second trimester, **bi-weekly visits**, and in the final trimester, **weekly check-ups**.

#### 2️⃣ Can I travel during pregnancy?

Yes, but it is safest to travel during the **second trimester (weeks 14-28)**. Always consult your doctor before making travel plans.

#### 3️⃣ What are the early signs of labor?

✔️ **Frequent contractions & back pain**.

✔️ **Water breaking**.

✔️ **Cervical dilation & pelvic pressure**.

#### 4️⃣ How can I prevent gestational diabetes?

Maintaining a **healthy diet, exercising regularly, and managing weight gain** can help reduce the risk of gestational diabetes.
